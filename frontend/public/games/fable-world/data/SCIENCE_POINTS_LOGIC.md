# Fable World: knowledge economy

Knowledge points buy blueprints but never replace the level gate. A player can choose a branch early, while late structures still require travelling, fighting, and gathering the matching materials.

## Awards

- Character level: +6 points.
- First capture of each species: +1 point.
- First landmark in a biome: +1 point.
- First victory over an alpha Fable: +2 points.
- First victory over a regional Warden: +5 points.

Repeated captures and ordinary kills grant XP, not knowledge. This prevents farming one safe spawn from unlocking the whole tree.

## Spend curve

- Levels 1–5: 0–2 points per blueprint. The player can afford most basics.
- Levels 6–12: 2–3 points. The player starts choosing between factory, food, combat, and capture power.
- Levels 13–20: 3–4 points. Cross-branch prerequisites make the expedition and the base depend on each other.
- Levels 21–30: 4–5 points. Rare materials and dangerous-biome access become the real gate.

With six points per level the whole tree is eventually affordable, but not immediately. Exploration bonuses smooth the curve and reward varied play without becoming mandatory.

## MVP telemetry to watch later

- Time to first capture: target 3 minutes.
- Time to first working Fable: target 7 minutes.
- Time to first automated resource: target 15 minutes.
- Time to enter the second biome: target 20 minutes.
- Percentage of players who understand the party/base choice without opening help.

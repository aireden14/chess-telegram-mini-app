# Carcassonne Tile PNG Prompts

`v1` was generated with the built-in image generation tool as one 6x4 atlas, then sliced into 256x256 PNG tiles. It looked painterly, but it was rejected for gameplay because the city and road geometry was not symmetric enough after rotations.

`v2` is generated deterministically by `../scripts/generate_symmetric_tiles.py`. It keeps the existing `RAW` tile logic from `index.html`, centers every road on side midpoints, and draws cities from symmetric masks so rotations stay readable.

`wizard3` keeps the same deterministic geometry but upgrades the art direction toward a gothic magical-school board-game look: deep emerald fields, parchment cobblestone roads, stone-walled towns, dark blue roofs, warm glowing windows, lanterns, towers, and shield accents. It intentionally avoids direct franchise logos, characters, text, or exact protected emblems.

`ai3d-v1` was generated by ChatGPT image generation as one 6x4 3D tile atlas, saved at `carcassonne-tile-atlas-ai3d-v1.png`, then sliced by `../scripts/slice_ai_tiles.py`. It keeps the same tile order as `RAW` in `index.html`, but it is not the active tile visual set because the generated city/road shapes were less readable in play.

Meeple role sprites are generated by ChatGPT image generation as a 5x3 3D miniature sprite sheet, saved at `../meeples/meeple-sheet-ai3d-v1.png`, then sliced and chroma-keyed by `../scripts/slice_ai_meeples.py`. Farmer sprites are generated as a separate 5x1 strip at `../meeples/farmer-sheet-ai3d-v1.png` and sliced by the same script:

- city feature: `knight-<player>.png`
- road feature: `robber-<player>.png`
- monastery feature: `mage-<player>.png`
- field feature: `farmer-<player>.png`

Each role has one transparent PNG per player color and follows the same gothic magical-board-game direction without direct franchise marks.

## Atlas Prompt

Use case: stylized-concept
Asset type: game asset sprite sheet, square board tiles for a medieval tile-laying board game
Primary request: Create one polished PNG atlas containing 24 separate square terrain tiles in a 6 columns x 4 rows grid, designed to be sliced into individual square PNG tiles.
Input images: the provided screenshot is a loose visual reference for top-down square board tiles with green fields, pale roads, golden walled towns, monasteries, riverside/grass detail, and readable tabletop-game silhouettes. Do not copy it exactly; create original art.
Style/medium: premium hand-painted digital board-game illustration, top-down orthographic view, crisp enough for 64px and 100px tiles, soft painterly texture, charming medieval/fantasy map style, rich grass texture, warm stone towns, pale winding roads, small buildings, tiny banners/shields, subtle inked edges.
Composition/framing: exact 6x4 sprite sheet; every cell is a perfect square with equal size; thin dark 4px gutters between cells for easy slicing; no perspective tilt; all features must meet cell edges cleanly at the center of each side when present.
Tile order, left-to-right top-to-bottom, one tile per cell: 1 monastery in field; 2 monastery with road entering from south; 3 complete city on all four edges with blue shield; 4 one city edge north; 5 city corridor north-south with blue shield; 6 city corridor north-south no shield; 7 two separate city caps north and south; 8 city corner north-east; 9 city corner north-east with shield; 10 three-edge city north-east-west; 11 three-edge city north-east-west with shield; 12 three-edge city north-east-west plus road from south; 13 same with shield; 14 city on east edge plus straight road north-south; 15 city on north edge plus road curve east-south; 16 city on north edge plus road curve south-west; 17 city on north edge plus three road ends east-south-west; 18 city corner north-east plus road curve south-west; 19 same with shield; 20 straight road north-south; 21 road curve south-west; 22 T junction roads east-south-west; 23 crossroads all four directions; 24 plain grassy field decorative filler.
Lighting/mood: bright readable board-game lighting, cheerful but not neon, subtle shadows inside art only.
Color palette: varied lush greens, parchment roads, honey-stone cities, terracotta roofs, blue shields, small flowers and earth accents; avoid one-note green-only look.
Constraints: no text, no numbers, no labels, no watermark, no meeples, no UI, no cards, no rounded outer sheet corners. Keep each tile square and isolated within its grid cell. Roads and cities must be centered on tile edges. Make it beautiful and usable as a sliced game asset atlas.

## Slice Notes

- Source atlas: `carcassonne-tile-atlas-v1.png`
- Cell size: `256x256`
- Grid: `6 columns x 4 rows`
- `roadC.png` was rotated 90 degrees clockwise after slicing to match base edges `S,W`.
- `roadT.png` was rotated 90 degrees counter-clockwise after slicing to match base edges `E,S,W`.
